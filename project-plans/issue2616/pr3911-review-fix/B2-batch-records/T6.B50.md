## provider-switch-transaction.test.ts (NOT migrated: unobservable read)

| # | scenario | assertions | outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `rejects replacement failure and restores the switching owner without disturbing a successful same-label sibling` | A's switch rejects with the held failure; A's `exportForStateSnapshot()`, `captureNamedParameters()`, active provider object and session client equal the pre-switch values; B's snapshot equals the literal `{ global: { activeProvider: 'replacement', 'base-url': ... }, providers: { fake: { model: undefined }, replacement: { ... } } }`; both agents stream a further turn and their histories contain `restored owner` and `committed sibling` | Kept (rollback and isolation; never Removed), blocked |

The scenario needs two things at once on a production route: (1) a client replacement that is held and then fails while sibling B commits, and (2) reads of the full `SettingsService` snapshot (`exportForStateSnapshot`) for both roots.

- Held and failing client replacement. `switchActiveProvider` calls `initializeClient` last (`provider-switch-execution.ts`), which is `SessionClientOwner.refreshAuth` and then `replaceSessionClient`. Its awaited steps are `extractExistingState`, `tokenizer.prepareTokenizer(provider, model)`, `prepareAgentClientReplacement` (`client.initialize` is lazy and calls no provider) and the ledger dispose. The only step a production input can hold and fail is `prepareTokenizer`, through `AgentConfig.tokenizerFactory`, which exists only on Route C (`createAgent`). `manager.setActiveProvider` is synchronous. Route A (`fromConfig`) and Route B (`prepareAgentPreflight`, `createForegroundAgent`) take no tokenizer or content-generator input.
- Snapshot reads. `exportForStateSnapshot()` is readable on Route A (the test's `SettingsService`) and Route B (`route.settings`, `preflight.captureSettingsSnapshot()`). On Route C the `Agent` exposes `getEphemeralSettings()`, `captureProfile()`, `getProviderStatus()` but not the provider-keyed snapshot, so B's literal assertion cannot be kept with unchanged values.
- Both of the `.settingsService` row's escape hatches fail together: Route C (a) gives the hold but not the snapshot, and moving to Route A (the row's fallback) loses the hold. Rows tried: tokenizer readiness, `.settingsService` and `internalConfig` reads, fault injected on the client, internal identity.

Decision needed (listed under "Unobservable reads" and in the handoff): rule one of (a) Route C with `tokenizerFactory` holding `prepareTokenizer('replacement', ...)`, the snapshot assertions replaced by `agent.getEphemeralSettings()`/`captureProfile()` equality for A and the sibling's public state for B (assertion change); (b) a `T6.Pk` that adds a public settings-snapshot read to `Agent`; or (c) accept the hold on Route B through a new Route B tokenizer input (a new public input, which the stop rule forbids the coder to add).
