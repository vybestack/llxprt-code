## T6.F26: event gates replace the polling deadlines

The review found that B102b replaced the recovery waits (1,000 ms default) with 5,000 ms history and admission polls and a 15,000 ms terminal-state poll, and that `awaitNewSessionAdmitted` retried `startNewSession()` until it stopped rejecting. These are gone, and the earlier lines of this record that describe them (the terminal wait through `agent.tasks.get(id).status`, `awaitNewSessionAdmitted` retrying `startNewSession()`) no longer apply.

- `awaitTerminal`, `awaitHistoryContains` and `awaitNewSessionAdmitted` are deleted. No `waitFor` in the file has a `timeout` option any more; the remaining `waitFor` calls use the default deadline they had before B102b and only wait for React state (`queuedSubmissions` length, `runnerReturned`/`runnerSettled`).
- The public place where background completion becomes observable is the notification subscription the hook registers through `agent.tasks.subscribeNotifications`. New helper `packages/cli/src/ui/hooks/agentStream/__tests__/helpers/notice-gates.ts` (`observeNotices(agent)`) wraps that method on the test's agent instance, delegating to the real one, and exposes three gates: `arrived(n)` (the owner handed the nth notice to the hook's `deliver`, which a suppressed or busy hook holds), `delivered(n)` (the nth `deliver` call settled, returned or thrown, so the submitted turn's history is already written), and `evaluated()` (the owner asked the hook whether the agent is busy, which it does when a completion arrives). Task and shell completion are awaited through them: `arrived`/`delivered` where a notice reaches the hook, `evaluated` for the busy-render cases that must prove the completion happened before asserting nothing was delivered (armed after one event-loop turn so the subscription's own startup evaluation does not count).
- History is asserted once after the gate (`expectHistoryContains`). The single admission switch is `admitNewSession`: one event-loop turn (the owner acknowledges a delivery on the microtasks after the hook returns; no timer) and then one `startNewSession()` call that is asserted to succeed. Nothing is retried, and an unrelated admission failure now fails at its cause.
- Timers kept as written: the `delay(350)` quiet periods. None was lengthened or added.
