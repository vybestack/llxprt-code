## Phase 6 review closure (T6.F25)

The identity lines `ownerAdopted` and `mediaAdopted` (pre-Agent client and its media store equal the agent's) are Removed as non-behavioral. `AgentActivationBootstrap.acquireActivationClient` (`packages/agents/src/api/fromConfig-session.ts:284-307`, called from `preparePostConfiguration` at `:195`) is the only session-client construction on Route B. It installs the client in the single field `ownedSessionClient` (`:307`, declared at `foreground-agent-assembly.ts:133`). `createForeground`/`finishForeground` construct no client; they read that field (`foreground-agent-assembly.ts:262`, `facadeSessionOwner = this.ownedSessionClient`). No allowed input can observe whether the agent's client is the pre-Agent one: `AgentPreflightHostInput` is data-only and zod-strict, `AgentForegroundHost` has no factories, contributions see no client or tokenizer factory, and no pre-Agent operation writes client-observable state. The earlier covering-assertion claims (history round trip, `Config.getContentGeneratorConfig()` identity) are withdrawn: both compared empty or `undefined` state on this route and could not fail. The "Covering read gap" section above is closed by this ruling; no `T6.Pk` or domain-level handoff test is needed. `providerAdopted` and the filesystem probe are unchanged.
