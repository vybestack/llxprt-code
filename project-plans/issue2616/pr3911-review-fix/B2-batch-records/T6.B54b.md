### Replaced test (names file and tests)

`packages/core/src/recording/RecordingIntegration.finalizerJoin.test.ts`, describe `RecordingIntegration finalizer join`, two tests (`failWrite` false and true):

- `retires its history listeners when disposal starts, joins accepted persistence, and ignores later additions`
- `retires its history listeners when disposal starts, joins accepted persistence and retains its original failure, and ignores later additions`

Built over a test-built `HistoryService`, a real `SessionRecordingService` (journal in a temp directory) and a `SessionPersistenceService` subclass whose `save` is held and then resolved or rejected by the test (the persistence boundary, as `RecordingIntegration.lifecycle.test.ts` does). Assertions: the four recording-event listener counts rise by one on `subscribeToHistory`; an accepted `history.add` schedules one persistence save; right after `dispose()` the counts are back at baseline while disposal is still pending after `setImmediate`; `dispose()` returns the same promise; once the save is settled disposal resolves (`failWrite = false`) or rejects with an `Error` whose `cause` is the injected failure (`failWrite = true`); a repeated `dispose()` returns the same outcome; the journal holds the accepted entry; a later `history.add` leaves the journal unchanged, schedules no further save and leaves the counts at baseline.
