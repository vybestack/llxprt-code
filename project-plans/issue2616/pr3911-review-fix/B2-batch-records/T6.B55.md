   - Rows tried: "Client calls that have an Agent equivalent" (no `Agent` member sends a non-stream message through `ChatSession.sendMessage`: `agent.chat()` is the stream drained into a result, `agent.generate()` goes through `generateDirectMessage` and a different processor, `DirectMessageProcessor`); `Agent.sessionClient` and `AgentActivationOperation.sessionClient` expose `fetch`, `runImageOperation`, `refreshAuth`, `publishTools` only. `git grep -n "sendMessage\b" -- packages ':!*test*' ':!*__tests__*'` finds no production caller of `AgentChatContract.sendMessage` (only its definition in `core/src/core/clientContract.ts`, `chatSession.ts:668` and `TurnProcessor.ts:177`).

   - Needs: the bus the agent's `HookControl` observes (`control/hooks.ts` subscribes to the request/response pair on the session bus).
   - Rows tried: ".messageBus used to answer or evaluate confirmations" (not a confirmation); "Internal identity" (c) (the bus is the medium the subject runs on, not an identity assertion; with a test-built `MessageBus` the sibling-transcript assertions would no longer involve the agent's `HookControl` at all); `Config` has no bus accessor; `Agent.hooks` has `triggerSessionStart/End`, `onHookExecution`, `listHooks` only.
