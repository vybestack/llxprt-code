## Unobservable reads

1. Scenario 2 (both cases), the session client's `HistoryService`: `listenerCount(event)` for four recording events, `history.on('removeListener', ...)` used as the "listeners retired" synchronization point, and `history.add(...)` called after the agent was disposed.
   - Needs: the live `HistoryService` of the agent's client. `listenerCount`, `on`/`off('removeListener')` and a producer that still adds after `agent.dispose()` have no `Agent` form (`agent.addHistory` rejects after disposal; the `Agent` has no history subscription).
   - Rows tried: "Client calls that have an `Agent` equivalent" (`getAll/add/clear` only, and `agent.addHistory` is rejected once disposed, so the late-producer check changes meaning); "Listener or subscription counts" (a domain-level test of the owner that subscribes; the subscriber here is the recording facade that only the `Agent` builds, and the scenario's subject is the `Agent` disposal order with a real lock, not the subscriber, so this would change what the scenario asserts); "Internal identity" (c) does not apply, because listener counts and the retirement point are the property under test, not a client identity; "Fault injected on the agent's client" does not apply (the fault, a held `fs.rename`, is already a real effect). `AgentActivationOperation.sessionClient` and `Agent.sessionClient` expose no history.
   - Decision needed: (c) with covering assertions the orchestrator names (the baseline-to-baseline listener counts and the `retired` gate carry the "listeners removed before the finalizer finishes" ordering; a real-effect replacement would have to say what plays that role), or Replaced by a domain-level test of `RecordingIntegration` over a `HistoryService` the test constructs (recording subscribers rise by one and are removed on disposal, a late `add` after disposal writes nothing) together with an agent-level scenario that keeps the held-`rename` assertions, naming file and test.

The file keeps scenario 2 (both cases) on the old harness; those cases are the only remaining users of the old harness in this file. A follow-up `T6.B54b` finishes them after the decision.
