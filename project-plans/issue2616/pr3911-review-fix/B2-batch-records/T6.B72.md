## Unobservable reads

1. `session.clearConversation` scenario 10 (`closes admission when client retirement fails`): one-shot `dispose` fault on the agent's published session client (`spyOn(previous, 'dispose')` running the original then throwing `retirement failed`), then asserting the agent reports the failure, keeps its session id and closes admission.
   - Needs: the instance of the session client the agent retires during `startNewSession()`.
   - Rows tried: "Fault injected on the agent's client" (a) a real cause: nothing a production input does makes the retired client's `dispose` reject after it ran; the subject is the agent's `startNewSession`, not one owner, so the domain-level form (`SessionClientOwner` over `openClientOwner`, as T6.B56 scenario 6) would test `SessionControl`'s retirement of the owner's client with a different subject than the agent-level admission-closure assertions (`stream` rejects `closed`, `setEphemeralSetting` throws `closed`), and `openClientOwner` yields no `SessionControl`/admission gate for those reads. Route A/B/C give no client seam; a prototype spy on `AgentClient.prototype.dispose` would be a new prototype spy.
   - Decision needed: (c)-style replacement by a covering assertion the orchestrator names, a domain-level `SessionControl` test it names, or approval of a one-shot `AgentClient.prototype.dispose` spy shaped like the file's existing `AgentClient.prototype.dispose` wrapper. The scenario stays on the old route (`withCapturedRecordedConversation`, `buildAgent`, `fixtureAgentResources`) until then (follow-up `T6.B72b`).
